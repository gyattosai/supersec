# 📢 Introducing the SuperSec Subject Dashboard: Your All-in-One Class Companion! 🚀

Hello everyone! 👋

To make tracking our class schedules, attendance, announcements, resources, and requirements much simpler and organized this semester, we are introducing our new **SuperSec Subject Dashboard**! 

No more digging through hundreds of backlogged messages in group chats or asking *"Did the professor post the slides?"* or *"Was I marked present today?"* Everything you need for our subjects is now centralized in one fast, mobile-friendly link.

---

## 🌟 What is the SuperSec Subject Dashboard?

The **SuperSec Subject Dashboard** is our official, view-only subject hub maintained by your class secretary. It is designed to give everyone quick, reliable, and up-to-date access to official class information without needing to log in or download an app.

---

## ⚡ What You Can Do With It

### 📅 1. Class Schedule & Real-Time Alerts
* **Meeting Schedules:** Check exact class days, times (in 12-hour AM/PM format), and professor information.
* **"No Class" & Suspension Notices:** See highlighted, immediate notices whenever classes are suspended, moved, or rescheduled, along with the official reason.

### 📋 2. Live Class Attendance & Transparency
* **Attendance Logs:** Review the official attendance breakdown (Present, Absent, Excused) after every class session.
* **Version History:** See when attendance records were published or updated.

### 🛡️ 3. Attendance Proof Submission (Missing from Attendance?)
* If you attended the lecture (e.g., via Zoom) but were accidentally missed or marked absent:
  1. Open the session's attendance link and tap **"Submit proof"**.
  2. Enter your name and upload your Zoom screenshot (JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, or AVIF up to 8 MB).
  3. Our verification system checks your proof and updates your record to **Present** (or queues it for a quick secretary review if verification needs confirmation).
  4. *Note:* Uploaded screenshots are private, secure, and used solely for attendance verification.

### 📢 4. Official Announcements & Reminders
* Access all official course announcements, project guidelines, exam schedules, and critical reminders in full formatting.
* Avoid missing important professor notices buried under group chat memes and chatter.

### 📚 5. Class Resources & Study Materials
* Find lecture slides, reading materials, PDFs, Google Drive links, and reference tools in one categorized list.
* Previews and direct links allow you to open or download what you need with a single click.

### ❓ 6. Searchable Q&A (Frequently Asked Questions)
* Have a question about a project requirement, deadline, or syllabus detail?
* Search through verified **Questions & Answers** by keyword or topic tag.
* Check whether an answer is an **Official** confirmed response or an **Unofficial** note from class discussions.

---

## 📱 How to Access It

1. **Click the Shared Subject Link:** Bookmark the link shared in our class chat (e.g., `https://<our-domain>/s/<public-id>`).
2. **No Account / Login Required:** You can immediately browse everything from your phone, tablet, or laptop.
3. **Toggle Light/Dark Theme:** Switch to your preferred viewing theme at any time.
